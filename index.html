What Did We Learn?

Key takeaways from Helen Keller's "Three Days to See"

❤️

1. Loved Ones First

Helen Keller valued the people she loved above everything else.

🌿

2. Beauty in Nature

She wanted to appreciate the miraculous sunrise, plants, and natural beauty.

🌍

3. The Wider World

She wanted to see city life, art in museums, and everyday working people.

👁️

4. Gratitude for Our Senses

The lesson teaches us to appreciate our sight, hearing, and senses every single day.
